# RAKSHA Hackathon Pitch & Demonstration Script

**Tagline**: *"Before You Pay. Verify."*

---

## Step-by-step Live Presentation Workflow

1. **Introduction (30s)**:
   - "Retail Indian investors across Tier-2/Tier-3 cities lose thousands of crores every year to WhatsApp and Telegram investment scams claiming 18% guaranteed monthly returns and fake SEBI registration numbers."
   - "RAKSHA intervenes BEFORE money changes hands."

2. **Live Solicitaton Demonstration (60s)**:
   - Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000` or click **Live Demo Suite**.
   - Select Scenario 1: **Fake SEBI Approved Guaranteed Investment Scam**.
   - Click **Run Live Investigation**.

3. **Show Evidence Breakdown (60s)**:
   - Point out the **CRITICAL / HIGH RISK** banner and calibrated scam probability.
   - Highlight **Why RAKSHA Flagged This**:
     - *Level 1 Authoritative Fact*: SEBI Registration `INZ000293847` unverified / mismatch.
     - *Level 4 ML Estimate*: Calibrated NLP Text Model (93% scam risk).
     - *Level 4 URL Estimate*: `sebi-pro-trader.top` flagged for suspicious TLD and APK download.
     - *Level 5 Red Flags*: Guaranteed return, urgency, Telegram migration, APK download.

4. **Show Evidence Graph & Timeline (30s)**:
   - Highlight how claims flow into official lookups and ML models to create an explainable graph.

5. **PDF Evidence Report & Recovery Mode (30s)**:
   - Click **Download PDF Evidence Report** to display the formal forensic evidence summary.
   - Click **Already Transferred Money?** to demonstrate emergency banking stop-payment and 1930 Cybercrime helpline guidance.
